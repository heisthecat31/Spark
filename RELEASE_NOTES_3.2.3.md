# Spark 3.2.3

## New
- **Music updates itself.** When you open the Music tab, Spark checks for a newer EchoVRMusic release (at most once per session). If there is one, a bar at the top of the tab offers **Update**. It downloads the new version, installs it into Echo VR, and reopens the tab. If Echo VR is running, it asks you to close it first.
- **EchoVRMusic now has version numbers**, starting at v0.1.0, released separately from Spark.

## Changes
- **Arena speakers moved 7.5 m closer in,** so the music in the arena is easier to hear and to place.
- **New link for the map testing Discord:** https://discord.gg/4VrpA95fMg

## Fixes
- **Music in the arena now comes only from the arena's speakers.** Echo keeps the main menu's music running in the background during a match, and that music used to play beside your head as well, drowning out the speakers. On maps with speakers it's now muted.
- **The Music tab always shows its settings once EchoVRMusic is installed.** A version mismatch used to swap the settings for an "Update" screen. It's now a small **Update** button in the corner ("Close Echo to update" while Echo VR is running).
